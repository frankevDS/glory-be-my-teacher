# Ghana SHS Subject Verification — NaCCA + WAEC

## Authority and scope

This catalog is based on the Ghana National Council for Curriculum and Assessment (NaCCA) **Subject Combination Guidelines for Secondary Education** and NaCCA's Secondary Education Curriculum resources, cross-checked against WAEC Ghana's WASSCE subject/examination information.

### Critical rule
A subject appearing in this catalog means it is recognized in Ghana's current curriculum and/or official WASSCE ecosystem. It does **not** mean every SHS learner must study it.

The current NaCCA model separates subjects into:

1. **Group A — Core**
2. **Group B — Learning Area Electives**
3. **Group C — Related Learning Area Electives**
4. **Group D — Other Electives**

Schools may also be constrained by facilities and staffing. NaCCA states that learners may exit with 7–9 externally assessed subjects and that additional subjects may be studied in Year 1 or up to Year 2 for internal assessment/transcripts. Robotics and Coding is mandatory for SHS 2 learners where facilities are available.

## Subjects every learner must treat as core in the new NaCCA structure

- Robotics and Coding — mandatory for all SHS 2 learners **if facilities are available**; internally assessed.

## Product implementation rule

Learn With Glory must store **curriculum year/version + subject + learning area + group + source + verification status**. A subject must not be treated as fully curriculum-grounded merely because its name appears in this catalog. Its actual NaCCA curriculum document must be ingested before strict curriculum teaching is enabled for that subject.
